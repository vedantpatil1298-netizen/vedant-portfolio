Who gets to change the plan?

When an older adult, a caretaker and a family member share a routine, they need different things from the same schedule. A caretaker can record what happened. A family member may just want to check in. The older adult should have a say in changes.

CareCircle gives each person their own role inside one care profile. Medication logs, appointments and proposed schedule changes stay attached to that profile.

Inside the build

A record of what happened

Due doses can be logged as taken or skipped. An unanswered entry stays unconfirmed, so families can distinguish a missing response from a recorded skip.

Questions grounded in the log

The care assistant can answer questions about the schedule, recent entries and upcoming appointments. A local intent classifier keeps those lookups available when hosted model routing is unavailable.

Permissions checked in the database

The repository tests role permissions, approvals and repeated dose logging against an isolated PostgreSQL database. These checks exercise the same schema and migrations used by the application.
